# Advancements

Part of the architecture, which is all in `index.html` (the Advancements section, after Effects).

Advancements are earned once each and kept across games, in the manner of Minecraft's. Each is in `ADVANCEMENTS`, by key: its group (`ADV_GROUPS`: Getting started, Skill, Speed, Keeping at it, Exploring, Items, Secret), name, how to get it, tier (`ADV_TIERS`: a task, goal or challenge, which gives its toast's heading, "Advancement Made!", "Goal Reached!" or "Challenge Complete!", its sound, the checkpoint chime, the win fanfare or the personal-best one, and its frame), the article whose picture is its icon, an emoji for when that picture hasn't loaded, and for a count its goal (`of`), what it counts (`tally`) and the unit. A secret one (`secret`) shows as ??? in the panel until earned: the Secret group, and Birdie among the skill ones. Adding one is an entry there and its check; it is earned from then on.

## What earns them

- *As a page arrives* (`advArrive`, from `navigate` right after the arrival is recorded, past its `try`, so it swallows its own errors): every arrival is counted (`visits`), and its title joins the different pages visited (`pages`) and its topic the topics seen (`topics`). Then First Click (any arrival after the start but Back), Herobrine and Cake (by title), Spin Doctor (a page in a spin-off's namespace), April Fools! (a page in the joke family), Around the World (every topic seen) and Full Circle (clicking back onto the start, not with Back, outside a Round trip).
- *As a game ends* (`advFinish`, from `finish`, given whether the pair had a game in the history before (`recordKey`, any rules) and whether it is a new personal best): a win counts (`wins`), then it waits for par (`game.parReady`, if the record doesn't have it yet) and every arrival's count (`measured`). What it needs of the game it reads before waiting, since Retry this pair can start another game in the meantime. Skill ones need a win at par `ADV_PAR` (3) or more on the pair's first game, so a typed easy pair or Retry this pair doesn't earn them, and speed ones the Timer too: Par for the Course (at par), Birdie (under par, which only a live edit making a route the offline copy lacks can do), Hardcore (`nohubs`, so Hard), No Take-Backs, Blindfolded, Six Shooter, Top Notch (each its rule), Long Way Round (Exact clicks at par + 5 or more), Grand Tour (a Round trip with three checkpoints), Kind Collector (a Scavenger hunt of kinds), Full Kit (Hard, No Back and Blind target together), Quick Thinker, Speedrunner and Sprinter (under 60 s, 30 s, and 45 s at par 4+). The rest: Hello, Wiki (a win), Daily Bread (any daily finished), Relay Racer, Treasure Hunter, Measure Twice and There and Back Again (a win at each game), Accepted (a win from a challenge link: `setup.challenge`, set when a link's cards load and cleared by `leaveDaily`, so by any change to them, and copied to `game.challenge`), Self-Improvement (a new personal best), Hot Streak (a win at par 4+ with Distance on, every click but those reaching a checkpoint or find, and the last, getting closer: `gotCloser`), Rags to Riches (a Race from Dirt to Diamond), So Close (out of Six clicks with the last page one click away) and Scenic Route (a win in 15 clicks or more).
- *As Distance cheers* (`cheer`): `game.cheerRun` counts the cheers in a row, set back by an arrival that didn't get closer, and three earn On a Roll.
- *Items* (see [items.md](items.md)): earned as the items are used, and Picking Up the Pieces at a game's end (a win with `game.rebuilt`).
- *Counts* are checked with every earning (`advEarn`): Regular, Veteran and Wiki Legend (`wins`), Bookworm (`visits`), Demolition Expert (`blasts`), Tourist and Cartographer (`pages`), and Habit, Week Streak and Devoted (`streak`: the daily streak now, `dailyStreaks`, so Clear history ends it).

## Toasts

`advEarn` queues each new one (`advQueue`), and `advPump` shows them in `#advToasts`, top right: up to `ADV_SHOWN` (3) at once, each for `ADV_MS` (5 s), the rest waiting for a place, with one sound for those shown together, the highest tier's. Each is Minecraft's own toast box (`Toast advancement.png`, at twice its 160 × 32) in every theme, with the item alone beside the heading, as in Minecraft. The heading is yellow, a challenge's purple. Until the box is in, a dark box like it. While playing they sit under the game header, so Back and Give up stay clear, and wait while an article loads (`frameReady` pumps them once it shows); a game's end shows its own `ADV_AFTER_RESULT` (1.2 s) after the result opens, once its fanfare has played, over the result. They slide in from the right, and with Reduce motion fade in instead. Above the effects' canvas and the result window, they take no clicks.

## Pictures

Each icon is its article's picture, asked for once with every other icon's, 50 titles a request (the API's limit) (`advPictures`, `prop=pageimages` at 96 px), when a toast or the panel first needs them, and kept in `advPics` by the title asked for (through the reply's normalisations and redirects). Until then, or if the request fails (it is asked again next time), the frame shows the emoji; when they come, every frame on the page (`.adv-frame[data-adv]`) is redrawn.

## The panel

The 🏆 Advancements button at the right of the Recent games heading (`#btnAdv`, with the count earned of all, `#advCount`) opens it (`openAdvancements`). Recent games shows when there are games or advancements earned; with advancements but no games (after Clear history), only its heading and the button. The panel's head stays at the top while the rest scrolls: the count and a bar, then the one picked (`advPicked`, `advDetail`): its frame, name and tier, how to get it, and when it was earned, or a count's progress ("7 of 50 wins"); an unearned secret says only "A secret. Keep playing.". Then each group's grid with its count and, for Skill and Speed, their rules: each advancement a tile with its frame and name, gold once earned, greyed out otherwise, with a small bar for a count. The frames are Minecraft's own (`Advancement-plain`, `-oval` and `-fancy`, for a task, a goal and a challenge), grey (`-raw`) until earned and gold (`-worn`) once it is. `advTextures` asks the wiki for them and the toast box with the pictures, and sets them as CSS variables (`--adv-plain-raw` … `--adv-toast`, and `adv-textured` on `:root` once the toast box is in). Until then the icons show with no frame. At the foot, Reset advancements (`#aReset`) asks first, then forgets everything in `mwr.advancements` (`advEmpty`).

## Storage

`mwr.advancements` holds `got` (each earned key and when, in ms), `wins`, `visits`, `blasts`, `pages` (titles, so a new snapshot keeps them) and `topics` (`WIKI_DATA.topics` keys). It is read once at load through `savedAdvancements`, which keeps only what `advEarn` would write: known keys with a number, whole counts of zero or more, strings, known topics; `adv` holds it, `pages` as a Set, and `saveAdv` writes it back. Clear history leaves it. It starts empty: nothing is worked out from games played before.
