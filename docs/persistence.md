# Persistence

Part of the architecture, which is all in `index.html`.

`localStorage` key `mwr.history` holds the last 50 results and is used for the recent-games table and the per-pair personal best. Storage is shared with every page on the origin and open to the player, so it is read through `savedGames()`, which leaves out anything `isRecord` doesn't accept as a record `finish` would write (the wrong types, a rule, topic or Content key that doesn't exist, or a daily that isn't a real day, though one after today is kept, so a clock or time zone change doesn't drop it). A Scavenger record has `finds` (its articles) or `kinds` (their categories) in place of `goal` (`isRecord` takes exactly one of the three, and `targetText` and `routeText` say them), and `recordKey` keys its personal best with its finds in any order. A lost record has the flag of its `LOSSES` key, `outOfClicks: true` (Six clicks or Exact clicks) or `tooSoon: true` (Exact clicks), an Exact clicks game has `exact` (its count), and a Checkpoint game has `vias` (the checkpoints' titles, in order), with `back` on a round trip when some come after the target. The whole `chain` (start, checkpoints, target, the checkpoints of the way back, and the start again for a round trip), an Exact clicks game's count (par + 2 and par + 4 on one pair are two games) and the rules are the personal-best key. Each record has `rules` (an array of rule keys), and the personal best only compares records with the same rules (`sameRules`): the fewest clicks, then the fastest time (`beats`). A game played with the Timer off has no `ms`, so it beats another only on clicks, and Recent games shows a dash for its time. A rolled target's `topic` and `reach` are recorded too, and so is `game.content` as `content` when anything was on, and `par` once known (none of them part of the personal-best key, since the cards already fix the game; `isRecord` only accepts `TOPICS` and `CONTENT` keys and a whole number for `par`). The result note shows the topic when it isn't Any and the Content as "Content: April Fools, Dungeons" (`settingsText`). Recent games (`renderHistory`) has five columns: the game as labels like the header's (the game, its extra rules, and a daily's day as a green "Daily 20 Oct"), the route (`routeText`) with the settings the labels don't say under it, the result (a badge, green for a win, orange for a loss, grey for giving up, with the golf name and par, or "Par 2" at par, under it, and Best on each pair's personal best), the time and the day (`whenText`: Today, Yesterday, then `shortDate`). The Clear history link above the table empties it, dailies included, after a confirmation dialog. `mwr.theme` holds the chosen theme, `mwr.sound` whether sound is on, `mwr.timer` whether the Timer is, `mwr.pathCollapsed` whether the path sidebar is collapsed (`setPathCollapsed`), and `mwr.background` the background's settings (`bgSet`: `style`, one of `BG_STYLES`, and the booleans `wave`, `route`, `roll` and `still`; anything else read from it is left out, so a field it lacks keeps its default). Access goes through the `store` wrapper, which swallows storage errors.
