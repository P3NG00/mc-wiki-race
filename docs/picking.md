# Picking articles

Part of the architecture, which is all in `index.html`.

Rolls draw titles from `wiki-data.js` (see [wiki-data.md](wiki-data.md)), which defines `WIKI_DATA`. `ARTICLES` maps every article and every spin-off page (titled `Dungeons:…`) to `{ links, body, topic, fams, tiers }`: how many pages link to it, how many of those from outside navboxes, its topic if it is in the pool, its Content (a family key such as `joke`, or its spin-off's name) and its tiers (`popular`, `deep`). Subpages, disambiguation, version and other non-content pages have neither topic nor Content, so only Chaos rolls them. `POOL` maps each topic (`TOPICS` holds their labels) to its articles and `POOL_TITLES` is their union. `CONTENT` maps each family key (`FAMILIES` holds their labels) and then each spin-off (`SPINOFFS`, name to namespace number) to the pages it adds, and `content()` lists the ones that are on, in that order. A rolled title is then looked up live with `pageInfo` for its description and picture, which also follows a redirect if the page has moved since the snapshot, and is rolled again if it no longer exists. Start and target, plus the checkpoints (`via0`…`via2`, as many as `viaKeys()` gives) under the Checkpoint rule, are rolled in parallel. `cardKeys()` lists the cards in use, in the order they are played, and `reroll('all')` rolls those and drops checkpoints not in use. `rollOne` reads the other cards after each await, so they normally don't land on the same article. If they do, `updatePlay` disables Start.
- *Pools* (the Pool row; [RESEARCH.md](../tools/wikidata/RESEARCH.md) has the testing behind them). Each card draws a group, then a member of it. `rollGroups` gives the groups: the five topics, then each family or spin-off that is on as one more group. It keeps them per pool, Content and whether the card needs links (`linked`, for a target or checkpoint, keeps only titles linked from at least `MIN_BACKLINKS` articles outside navboxes). `planGroups` picks a different group for each card, uniformly, so the start and target are never from the same topic (a topic's navbox would put them one click apart), and gives the target the chosen topic first. `groupOf` reads a card's group from its page, which also covers custom, challenge-link and daily cards. A custom article may be anything.
  - *Popular*: the best-linked 30% of the pool (`tiers` has `popular`), from Lava, Fire and Villager down to about Andesite and Deep Dark. It is the relaxed pool: its pairs have the most routes.
  - *Everyday*, the default: the whole pool.
  - *Deep cuts*: the least-linked third of each topic, among articles linked well enough to be a target (`deep`), such as Camel Husk or Curse of Binding. Mobs are all well known, so a few are in Popular too.
  - The topics are uneven (blocks 478, items 273, mobs 93, world 94, magic 82), so drawing a topic first gives each a fifth of the rolls. A family or spin-off that is on is one more group, so Dungeons (1,364 pages) gets the same share as a topic.
- *Chaos*: uniform over `CHAOS_TITLES`, every article in the data (render-history subpages, disambiguation and non-content pages included, but not the version and list pages, which the data leaves out), plus every page of each spin-off that is on (`SPINOFF_PAGES`). Its cards may share a topic. Its targets and checkpoints come from the half linked from at least `MIN_BACKLINKS` articles outside navboxes, since nothing could reach the rest.
- *Content* is the Content row (the families) and the Spin-offs row, with All (`syncAllSpinoffs` ticks it when every spin-off is on and shows a dash when only some are). In Chaos the families are in anyway, so `syncContentToMode` shows their boxes ticked and disabled, and gives back what they were on leaving Chaos. `init` runs it too, since a reload can bring the Pool radio button back on Chaos. Changing Content rerolls every card, as changing the pool does, which leaves a daily. `loadDaily` turns all Content off (`setContent([])`), since a daily is its pair and rule only.
- *Target topic* (`TOPICS`, the select in the Target row) pins the target's group to that topic in every pool, Chaos included (there it draws from the whole topic). A new topic rerolls the target, and in the pool modes the start too if it is from that topic. `rollOne` stamps a rolled target with `topic`, and `finish` copies it into the record. Targets that weren't rolled as targets (custom, challenge link, daily, swapped in) have none. The topic isn't carried in challenge links, since the link already fixes the pair.
- *Target par* (`REACHES`, `reach()`, the Target row's second select, hidden under Scavenger): Any par, Par 1 or Par 3+, how many clicks the target is from the start under the difficulty and Content. Most pool pairs are par 2 and none is more than 4 (on the 2026-08 snapshot, par 3 is 14% of targets in Normal and 31% in Hard, where a few pairs are par 4, and 1% in Relaxed, where only a third of starts have a par 3 target), so these are the one-click targets and the far ones. With a par chosen, `reroll` rolls the start first, `rollOne`'s `accept` taking one only if some target is at the par from it, then the other cards in the topics it leaves (`planGroups` again), then the target from those at the par from it (`titles`), all before any lookup. Rolling the checkpoints first lost the one topic with a start to a checkpoint on most Relay rolls of three. Checking a start only needs par 1, 2 and further told apart, so `distancesFrom` searches just two clicks deep from it (`depth`), five times quicker than the whole graph, and any target further counts as par 3 if the web of links reaches it (`WEB_HEART`'s own search), since a rolled start joins the web too. The target is drawn by the start's whole search, since a start typed in hasn't been checked to join it. The start comes from each topic no other card holds (nor the target topic), in random order (`shuffled`), drawn at random and then from the rest of the topic, which random draws can miss when few fit: a whole topic can lack one (in Popular on Relaxed only six starts have a par 3 target, none of them Blocks or Mobs, so stopping at one topic would often give up). Chaos, the whole wiki in one group, only draws. A roll never ends without a game: when no start has one (Popular on Relaxed has no Blocks target at par 3+ from any of its starts, and none at all for Mobs or Enchantments & effects, even from Everyday's starts), Target par goes back to Any, the roll goes on without it, and the status line says why. Unless a checkpoint kept from before held a topic, or Chaos only drew, that is certain, so the option is disabled, with the reason in its tooltip, while the pool, rules, Content and topic stay the same (`noPair`, `pairKey`, `syncReach` on focus). Drawing the target at random instead would miss: in Relaxed a start may have one or two par 3 targets among a thousand. Unless a topic is chosen the target may then come from any topic but the start's (`candidates`), since some topics have no far targets from most starts. Picking a par or topic rolls the start again too (the `#target` listener), unless it was typed in (`typed`, set by `setCustom`): that one is kept, and with no target at the par it gets one at the par nearest the asked one (`near`) and says so, as a start kept through another difficulty or Content does when only the target is rerolled. Only a start that reaches nothing gets no target, and the status line asks for another. Rerolling only the start keeps the target (`kept`): the start is drawn from those at the par from it, found by one search back from the target (`distancesTo`), or at the nearest par if none is, and says so. Like the topic, it is stamped on a rolled target (`reach`), shown in the settings text unless Any, and not carried in challenge links. It is recorded only if the game's par still fits it, since a new start or difficulty can move the pair off it.
- A target or checkpoint must be linked from at least `MIN_BACKLINKS` articles outside navboxes (`body`), so No navboxes never gets one that only navboxes link to, and all cards must differ. The topic applies only to the target.
- Every rolled card must also join the wiki's web of links, by the link graph under the difficulty and Content (`joinsWeb`, before each lookup): a start needs a route to `WEB_HEART` (the pool article linked from the most articles outside navboxes that isn't a hub, Lava), a target a route from it, and a checkpoint, or either end of a round trip, both, so the cards always have a route between them (a round trip's way back may still only retrace, which `stranded` covers). Without it Chaos rolled about 3% of starts with no way out (Bee Our Guest links only hubs, dead in Hard; most are subpages that link nowhere), and a spin-off could roll a target nothing reaches. A page the snapshot lacks passes, and so does every page if the graph can't load, so every roll waits for the graph. Custom articles and challenge links aren't checked, nor blocked on the setup screen, since a live edit may have given them a route since the snapshot, and the daily has its own route check.
- *Daily* (`loadDaily`, `dailyPair`): a local date fixes a pair and one rule. The pair comes from `POOL_TITLES`, whatever the pool and topic, with the target (and the checkpoints) from a topic no card before it has, so a new `wiki-data.js` can change past days' pairs. Every title is ranked by `hash32("date|start|title")` (or `|target|`), and the lowest-ranked one that passes the usual checks wins. With rendezvous hashing like this, pages joining or leaving the categories only change a day's pair if they would rank first, which re-indexing a sorted list would not give. Every daily plays on Normal plus one rule (`dailyRule`), ranked the same way over `DAILY_RULES` (salt `rule`): No Back, Blind target, Checkpoint, No hubs (which makes it Hard), Six clicks, Round trip (a Race there and back), Exact clicks or Scavenger. An Exact clicks day only takes a target with a route of exactly its count (`exactRoute`, with no Content), and a Scavenger day ranks its mode over `DAILY_HUNTS` (salt `hunt`): kinds are the first in the day's ranking (salt `kind`) from three topics, none holding the start, and articles are the target and two more taken like checkpoints, needing a route in any order. A kinds daily has no target, so `init` checks `cardsLoaded()`, not the target, before rolling a pair instead. A daily can always be done: `dailyPair` only takes a target (and each checkpoint, after the ones before it) where the link graph has a route under the day's rules, and on a Six clicks day one of `DAILY_SIX_CLICKS_PAR` (4) clicks or fewer, leaving two for a wrong turn. That check comes before each lookup, so it costs no requests, but the daily needs the graph and fails to load without it. On a Checkpoint day `dailyPair` also ranks the count over `DAILY_CHECKPOINTS` (1 or 2, salt `checkpoints`: three would use every topic and make a long daily) and picks the checkpoints (salts `via`, `via1`). `loadDaily` replaces the ticked rules with the day's. `setup.daily` holds the date while the cards and rules are the day's, and any reroll, custom article, swap or rule change clears it (`leaveDaily`) and shows the same notice (`LEFT_DAILY`), so a daily always means that pair with that rule. The game copies it to `game.daily`, and the record stores it as `daily`.
