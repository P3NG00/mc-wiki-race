# The setup screen

The setup screen puts playing first. Beside the title are Sound, Timer, Reduce motion, Theme and the Background box (see [ui.md](ui.md)). Top to bottom: the daily (`.daily-box`: today's game in words, from `dailyText`, how you did if you won it, and a stats line, `#dailyStats`, with the streak, the best streak and the days won (see [persistence.md](persistence.md)), all from `renderDailyBox`, called by `renderHistory`; Play the daily is `#btnDaily`, and Past dailies beside the stats, `#btnPastDailies`, opens a calendar, `openCalendar`: a month at a time from `DAILY_FIRST`'s to this one, the week starting on the locale's first day, each day from `DAILY_FIRST` to today a button that loads its daily (`loadDaily`), named with its game and result in its tooltip, and marked won on its day, won later or not won), Your game (`#gameSum`, filled by `updateGameSum`: the game with its option, the extra rules, the articles and the difficulty), the cards, Start game and par, Reroll all, and Random game (`#btnRandom`, `randomGame`: every setting under the cards at random, each on its own, every box a coin flip and every choice as likely as the others, then `reroll('all')`; Sound, Timer, Theme and the background's settings stay as they are, but Roll by itself, which any change turns off), and then, below a divider (`.game-settings`), the steps: 1 · Choose a game (the tiles, `GAMES`, a radio group named `game`, read by `gameMode()`, with the strip under them, `#gameHelp` plus the picked game's own options, `.game-opt`: the checkpoint count, What to find, and Exact clicks's clicks over par, `#exactExtra`, with its count beside it, `#exactInfo`, filled by `updateSetupPar`), 2 · Extra rules (`EXTRA_RULES`, boxes with a description each, in `#rules`, Round trip first), 3 · Articles and difficulty (`#settings`, always shown: the pool, the difficulty, the target's topic and par, Content and Spin-offs). On a phone the cards lose their minimum height and show two lines of description, so Start game fits on the first screen. The screen holds still under the pointer: what was last clicked or changed on it (`keep`, set by a capture listener on `#setup`) stays where it is in the window. A ResizeObserver on its blocks and cards (`keepPlace`) scrolls the page, before it is drawn, by as much as anything above it changed size (the line above the cards, the cards as they roll and their pictures come in, Start's row, the strip's help). When the page gets shorter under it at the bottom, room under the footer (`#setupRoom`) keeps it long enough, and that room goes as the page is scrolled up out of it. A click on a box's text when the box itself is above the window scrolls back to where the click was, undoing the browser's scroll to the focused box. A card emptied for a roll keeps its height until its article is in (`renderCard`, which marks it `.empty`). While a roll or lookup is under way (`withBusy` sets `#setup.busy`) the status line is bold with a spinner, and each emptied card has a spinner by its tag and says it is picking an article; `reroll` lets the page be drawn that way (`painted`) before its searches, which can hold the page up for a moment. Par has its own spinner while it is worked out (see [par.md](par.md)). The spinners are transform animations, so they keep turning while a search runs, and turn slowly with reduced motion. While something is held, the browser's own scroll anchoring is off (`#setup.keeping`), since it would hold a different element and its scrolling would read as the player's. Scrolling the page lets go (a scroll up at the bottom of the page is the browser clamping to a page that got shorter, so it is held again), and so do resizing the window and leaving the screen. A round button in the bottom right corner (`#btnBgView`) hides it all but the background and its settings (see [ui.md](ui.md)). It ends with the facts line (`#bgFacts`, see [ui.md](ui.md)) and a footer (who runs it, a contact address and Discord profile, where to report a bug or an idea: an email link, `#feedbackMail`, whose click fills the mail's body in with the game on the cards as its link, `gameLink`, the last game played with its route, the offline copy's date and the browser (copied or opened some other way, it has only the subject), and GitHub's new issue page; the GitHub link, MIT license, credit for the wiki's CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 content, the date of the wiki's offline copy the rolls and par go by, `#wikiCopy`, filled from `WIKI_DATA.snapshot` by `init` and linking Minecraft Wiki:Offline use, a non-affiliation notice). That licence asks reusers to link the pages they show, so during play the bottom of the path sidebar (`#credit`, set in `navigate`) links the current article on minecraft.wiki (`wikiUrl`) and the licence (collapsing the sidebar leaves it in a small box over the article's corner, with the button that brings the sidebar back), and the result dialog links every page in the path. Without JavaScript, a `<noscript>` style in `<head>` hides the setup controls and the footer's date line, and a `<noscript>` notice under the intro explains why.
