# The setup screen

The setup screen puts playing first. Beside the title are Sound, Timer, Theme and the Background box (see [ui.md](ui.md)). Top to bottom: the daily (`.daily-box`: today's game in words, from `dailyText`, and how you did if you won it, from `renderDailyBox`, called by `renderHistory`; Play the daily is `#btnDaily`), Your game (`#gameSum`, filled by `updateGameSum`: the game with its option, the extra rules, the articles and the difficulty), the cards, Start game and par, Reroll all, and Random game (`#btnRandom`, `randomGame`: every setting under the cards at random, each on its own, every box a coin flip and every choice as likely as the others, then `reroll('all')`; Sound, Timer, Theme and the background's settings stay as they are, but Roll by itself, which any change turns off), and then, below a divider (`.game-settings`), the steps: 1 · Choose a game (the tiles, `GAMES`, a radio group named `game`, read by `gameMode()`, with the strip under them, `#gameHelp` plus the picked game's own options, `.game-opt`: the checkpoint count, What to find, and Exact clicks's clicks over par, `#exactExtra`, with its count beside it, `#exactInfo`, filled by `updateSetupPar`), 2 · Extra rules (`EXTRA_RULES`, boxes with a description each, in `#rules`, Round trip first), 3 · Articles and difficulty (`#settings`, always shown: the pool, the difficulty, the target's topic and par, Content and Spin-offs). On a phone the cards lose their minimum height and show two lines of description, so Start game fits on the first screen. It ends with the facts line (`#bgFacts`, see [ui.md](ui.md)) and a footer (who runs it and a contact address, GitHub link, MIT license, credit for the wiki's CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 content, the date of the wiki's offline copy the rolls and par go by, `#wikiCopy`, filled from `WIKI_DATA.snapshot` by `init` and linking Minecraft Wiki:Offline use, a non-affiliation notice). That licence asks reusers to link the pages they show, so during play the bottom of the path sidebar (`#credit`, set in `navigate`) links the current article on minecraft.wiki (`wikiUrl`) and the licence (collapsing the sidebar leaves it in a small box over the article's corner, with the button that brings the sidebar back), and the result dialog links every page in the path. Without JavaScript, a `<noscript>` style in `<head>` hides the setup controls and the footer's date line, and a `<noscript>` notice under the intro explains why.
